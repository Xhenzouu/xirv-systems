XIRV Systems is an enterprise intelligence platform designed to help organizations manage their knowledge and leverage artificial intelligence.

The platform includes several key features:
1. Document Management - Upload, organize, and search documents
2. AI Chat - Conversational AI powered by Ollama
3. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) - Answer questions based on your documents
4. Knowledge Base - Structured knowledge repository

XIRV Systems uses a modern technology stack including Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma, and Ollama for local AI inference.
